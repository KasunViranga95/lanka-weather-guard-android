# Lanka Weather Guard – Privacy policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

Lanka Weather Guard shows weather forecasts, river levels and flood and weather alerts for places in Sri Lanka. This policy explains what the app does with information about you.

## The short version

- No account, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics.
- Your settings and saved places stay on your phone.
- To get forecasts, the app sends the **coordinates of the town you choose** to the weather service. It never sends your exact GPS position.

## Location

If you tap **Use my location**, the app asks Android for your last known location. It uses that only on your phone to find the nearest town in its list. It then uses that town's coordinates, not your own position, to request the forecast. The app does not track your location in the background.

You can always choose a town by hand instead.

## Information sent to other services

| Service | Why | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) | Weather forecasts | Coordinates of your chosen towns |
| Sri Lanka Irrigation Department river gauge service (ArcGIS) | River levels | A request for the latest gauge readings (nothing about you) |
| Sri Lanka Water Levels (waterlevels.lk) | HMIS and RiverNet.lk river gauges, reservoir reports | A request for the latest readings (nothing about you) |
| Windy.com | The live map on the Map tab | The map position of your chosen town. Windy.com may use its own cookies; see its privacy policy. |
| AI helper (only if you use it) | Answers to your questions | Your question, plus the current weather and river data for your chosen town. It goes through the app's own server to Google Gemini. Do not type personal information into the AI helper. |

These services receive your phone's IP address as part of any internet request.

## Information stored on your phone

- Your saved places and the place currently shown
- Settings: language, theme, alert notifications on or off, and your flood checklist ticks
- The last forecast and river readings, so the app works when you are offline
- Which alerts have already been sent to you, so you get each one only once

All of this is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Notifications

If alert notifications are on, the app checks about every 30 minutes for your saved places and notifies you about new or worsening alerts. You can turn this off in **Settings** or in the **Safety** tab.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
